# BOXES Framework

## 1. B - Brain (Strategy Box)
This is the first box which represents the brain for the idea, this is the strategy box.

- **Real & Painful Pain Point**: Identify the real pain point, or problem we are trying to solve
- **Clear Customer Persona**: Who will be using the solution we are presenting
- **Distinct Competetive Edge**: That we are providing
- **Supporting Trend (AI / Rising Market)**: How to use AI or new trends in the solution
- **Prelimnary Business Model**: Building a business model to enable and simplify the solution

### Prupose
The goal from this box is to take a clear, and informed decision before starting building the solutiuon. To confirm that the idea is feasable and worth buidling a product before spending time, effort, and money.

### Outcome
- Confirm that there is a real effective problem
- Precisely define the client who is able to pay for it
- Confirm that the market is active and there is a real demand
- Define a clear and different competetive advantage
- Test the feasibility and enablement of making money
